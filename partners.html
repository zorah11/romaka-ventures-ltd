Current collaborators

Partners close to the community.

NIRP

Ndibwami Integrated Rescue Project

A community-based organisation supporting development, advocacy, smallholder farmers and people living with HIV and AIDS. NIRP mobilises and trains farming households in Kyanamukaka and Buwunga sub-counties.

KACS

Kyanamukaka Allied Cooperative Savings & Credit Society

The cooperative helps members access loans for agricultural inputs. Romaka supports the relationship by paying farmers’ produce proceeds through their cooperative accounts.
