Our story

Local produce, shared progress.

Incorporated in 2022, Romaka was created to develop business from horticultural produce in Uganda’s central region. We work with pineapples, watermelons, green peppers, passion fruits and mangoes.

Our pineapple-jam initiative is designed to help more than 1,000 smallholder farmers earn higher incomes. Stronger farm incomes help families afford balanced diets, school fees and essential healthcare.

Our partners

Collaboration that reaches farming households.
