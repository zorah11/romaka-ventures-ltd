# Romaka Ventures Ltd — sample website

A responsive eight-page site presenting Romaka as a horticultural agribusiness and farmer-support company in Masaka, Uganda. It includes dedicated pages for products, farmers, impact, partners, capabilities, company information and contact.

The company information, contact details, impact figures and photography are based on the supplied company profile. The logo has responsive transparent SVG variants, and the contact form opens a pre-addressed email in the visitor's email application.
